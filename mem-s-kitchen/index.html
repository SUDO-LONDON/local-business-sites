About Mem's Kitchen

At Mem's Kitchen, we believe that food is more than just sustenance—it's a celebration of love, tradition, and community. Our restaurant stands as a beacon of warmth in the heart of Wickford, serving authentic, home-style cooking that touches the soul.

Every dish is crafted with the same care and passion that Mem brought to her kitchen generations ago. We use fresh, quality ingredients and time-honored recipes to create meals that feel like coming home.
