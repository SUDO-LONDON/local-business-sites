About Mem's Kitchen

At Mem's Kitchen, we believe that great food brings people together. Located in the heart of Wickford, our family-run restaurant has been serving the community with authentic, home-style cooking that reminds you of meals shared around the family table. Using fresh, quality ingredients and time-honored recipes, we create dishes that comfort the soul and satisfy the heart.

Every dish that leaves our kitchen carries with it the warmth and care that defines who we are. We're not just serving food—we're sharing stories, traditions, and the love that goes into every bite.
