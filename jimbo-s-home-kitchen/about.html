About Jimbo's

Our Story of Love, Care & Home Cooking

About Jimbo's Home Kitchen

At Jimbo's Home Kitchen, we believe the best meals are made with love, care, and the finest ingredients. Located in the heart of Wickford, we've been serving our community with traditional home-style cooking that brings families together around the table.

Every dish that leaves our kitchen carries the warmth and comfort of home. We use only fresh, quality ingredients sourced from trusted local suppliers, ensuring that every bite tastes like it came from a grandma's kitchen—because it's made with that same dedication and love.

Our Mission & Values

Quality First

We never compromise on the quality of ingredients or the care we put into every meal.

Community

We're more than a restaurant—we're a gathering place where families and friends create memories together.

Passion

Every recipe, every technique, every plate is prepared with genuine passion for the craft of cooking.

Meet the Team

Jimbo

Jimbo is the heart and soul of Jimbo's Home Kitchen. With over 20 years of culinary passion and a deep love for authentic home cooking, he founded this restaurant with a simple mission: to serve meals that feel like they're made by family.

Drawing inspiration from cherished family recipes and a commitment to local, quality ingredients, Jimbo brings warmth and hospitality to every dish that leaves the kitchen. His belief is simple—good food shared with good people is the best medicine.
