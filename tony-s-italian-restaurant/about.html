About Tony's Italian Restaurant

For over two decades, Tony's Italian Restaurant has been serving the Wickford community with genuine Italian hospitality and time-honoured recipes. Our family-run restaurant brings the warmth of Italy to High Street, where every dish tells a story of tradition, passion, and authentic flavours crafted with the finest ingredients.

Located at 52 High Street, Wickford SS12 9AT, we are proud to be a cornerstone of the community, welcoming families, friends, and food lovers who appreciate the true taste of Italy.

Our Mission & Values

Authentic Tradition

We honour the time-tested recipes passed down through generations, bringing the authentic taste of Italy to every plate served at our table.

Family Values

Our family-run restaurant embodies warmth, hospitality, and genuine care. We treat every guest as part of our extended family.

Quality Ingredients

We source only the finest ingredients to create dishes that celebrate simplicity and flavour in the truest Italian tradition.

Our Team
